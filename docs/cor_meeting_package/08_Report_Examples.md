# Report Examples — what the Government receives

| Deliverable | Contract | Frequency | Format proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **3.1 Weekly Progress Report** | Task 3 | Weekly | Stratified list across the four Government categories, plus methodology changes suggested or implemented. **In delivery since July 2026** |
| **3.2 Final Report** | Task 3 | 30 days after the 120-day review completes | Aggregated 120-day data, stratified; sampling methodology and confidence calculation; all methodology changes |
| **4.1 Bi-Weekly Progress Report** | Task 4 | Bi-weekly | Stratified list for new entrants in the period |
| **4.2 Quarterly Report** | Task 4 | Each calendar quarter | Aggregated 90-day overview |
| **5.1 Status Report** | Task 5 | At COR direction | Concise: identified issue, root cause if determined, severity — one review per Participant or Sub-participant |
| **5.2 Quarterly Report** | Task 5 | Each calendar quarter | Aggregated 90-day priority-review overview |
| **6.1 Closeout Report** | Task 6 | Agreed date within 90 days | Methodologies, framework, tools, files and data produced |
| **6.2 Educational Presentation** | Task 6 | Agreed date within 90 days | Briefing conveying the closeout report |

## Common content

Every report states: the contract and task, the review period, the population and
where applicable the sample and its confidence calculation, the sources and their
editions, source limitations, the stratified results in Government terminology,
analyst and QA status, and any COR decision affecting interpretation.

## Presentation principles

Priority reviews are concise and operational — one page a reader can act on.
The retrospective carries population, sampling methodology and aggregate
findings. Reports are not forced into a single template, and internal software
architecture does not appear in a Government deliverable.

Every figure carries its denominator. Observation counts, entity counts, finding
counts and QA-approved counts are reported separately and are never substituted
for one another.
