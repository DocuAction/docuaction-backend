# Discrepancy Classification Crosswalk

The four categories are the Government's, stated in Section C, Tasks 3 and 4.
AGT's internal shorthand maps one-to-one onto them. **Government-facing reports
use the Government's wording.**

| Government category (§C) | Contract definition | Internal label | Analyst action | QA action | Report wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1) No discrepancies identified** | Sources confirm the submitted information | B1 | Confirm all applicable sources answered | Verify evidence sufficiency | "No discrepancies identified" |
| **2) Minor or administrative discrepancies** | A difference not indicating non-compliance — for example an address format difference or name spelling variation | B2 | Document; QHIN notified of the administrative correction | Verify "minor" is supported by the evidence | "Minor / administrative discrepancy" |
| **3) Inexplicable discrepancies** | A difference not explained by simple administrative error; the QHIN must investigate and provide a documented response | B3 | Document findings; corrective action required per the Common Agreement | Verify an explanation was genuinely sought — **highest return rate expected** | "Inexplicable discrepancy" |
| **4) Non-compliant discrepancies** | A difference indicating non-conformance | B4 | Highest scrutiny; must be fully evidenced | Independent verification before approval | "Non-compliant discrepancy" |

**Status.** D2 records that AGT and ONC will **collaboratively finalise** the
taxonomy; the definitions above are AGT's proposed approach pending COR
acceptance. The four category names themselves are contractual and are not
AGT's to change.

**A category is assigned by a person.** Automated observation feeds the analyst;
it does not classify the entity.
