## Agenda

### 1 · Purpose and current readiness — 5 minutes

Where AGT stands: methodology submitted 9 July, resubmitted in Word 27 July;
weekly reports Nos. 1–6 delivered; platform certified end to end on development
data; **0 of 383 entities reviewed**, because the assignment and entity data have
not been received.

No decisions sought. Sets the frame: nothing is blocked on AGT.

### 2 · Verification workflow — 10 minutes

The path from an entity record to a reported result: intake and preservation,
source verification, discrepancy identification, analyst determination,
independent QA, reportability.

**The point to land:** a system observation is not a Government finding. A
result becomes reportable only through analyst determination followed by
independent QA approval by a different person.

*Decision sought:* none. Confirmation that the workflow matches the COR's
expectation.

### 3 · Evidence sources — 10 minutes

NPPES, CMS PECOS/PPEF, OIG LEIE, SAM.gov, CMS Revocation, USPS. What each
establishes, what it does not, and what happens when one cannot answer.

**Two points to land:**

- An unavailable source is a fact about the lookup, never about the entity.
- **SAM.gov cannot currently be queried** — AGT holds no credential. This
  affects every entity and needs a Government route to resolution.

*Decisions sought:* **D4** (unavailable source treatment); SAM.gov access.

### 4 · Sampling methodology — 10 minutes

95% confidence is the contract floor. AGT proposes ±5%, giving 383 entities from
94,231, stratified across the QHINs with finite population correction, drawn
reproducibly from a recorded seed.

**The point to land:** the margin of error is what determines workload. ±5%
gives 383; ±3% gives roughly 1,060. The contract fixes the confidence level and
is silent on the margin.

*Decisions sought:* **S1** population of record · **S4** margin of error ·
**S5** replacement policy.

### 5 · Discrepancy methodology — 10 minutes

The four Government categories and how evidence maps to each. What the analyst
verifies, what QA verifies, and which conditions cannot yet be categorised.

**The point to land — D4-ADDR.** The entity record carries a *registered*
address; NPPES and PECOS publish *practice locations*. These differ legitimately
for compliant organisations. Development-data validation demonstrated that
address comparison rules **materially affect review volume**, so AGT requests
confirmation of the address materiality methodology before applying it to
Government-authorised data.

*Decision sought:* **D4-ADDR**. This is the single highest-value decision in the
meeting.

### 6 · Remaining methodology decisions — 10 minutes

D1, D2, D3, D5, D6, D7, D8, D9 — each with AGT's recommendation and the
operational effect. Six have a safe conservative default AGT can apply; **D1,
D5 and D7 do not**.

*Decisions sought:* as many as time allows. Any not reached are taken in writing.

### 7 · Data transfer and next actions — 5 minutes

What AGT needs to start: the Government assignment, the entity population, and
the transfer mechanism. Confirmation of whether the HHS Data Access Agreement
gates delivery.

*Decisions sought:* **T1** transfer mechanism · **T2** Data Access Agreement
status · target delivery date.
