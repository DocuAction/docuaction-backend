---

## Preconditions

This plan begins on **Day 1 = the later of** written COR acceptance of the D2
methodology **and** receipt of the authorised entity population. Both are
Government actions. Nothing here can start before both are in hand.

**Schedule context.** Task 3's 120-day retrospective window runs from award,
placing completion at approximately **23 October 2026**, with the D3.2 final
report due thirty days after that. The plan below is written to be compressed
where the remaining window requires it, and Week 4 records the trade-offs
honestly rather than assuming they can be absorbed.

| Day | Activity | Output |

| 1 | Record the population unaltered; compute its integrity value; confirm the record count against the Government's control total | Receipt confirmation to the COR, with any variance stated |

has been resolved, every report will carry that limitation.

## What is deliberately not in this plan

- **No Task 4 activity.** Ongoing review addresses new entrants and begins on its
  own cadence once Task 3 is under way.
- **No findings issued to the Government before QA approval.** A result reaches a
  deliverable only through the full controlled path.
- **No methodology change made unilaterally.** Where AGT proposes one, it is
  reported and applied only if the COR authorises it.
- **No census claim.** If AGT screens beyond the sample, it will be reported as
  supplementary coverage, not as satisfying the contractual sample.

---

## Risks AGT is tracking

| Risk | Effect | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Schedule compression** | The later the data arrives, the less of the 120-day window remains | AGT can begin within days of receipt; the constraint becomes analyst throughput, and AGT will state plainly what a given window supports rather than promise a volume it cannot review properly |
| **SAM.gov access unresolved** | One verification source unavailable across the whole population | Disclosed in every report; AGT requests a Government route to access |
| **Open methodology decisions** | Affected conditions cannot be categorised | Counted and disclosed as awaiting methodology; never defaulted |
| **QA capacity** | QA is the throughput constraint, not analysis | QA reviewer identified at assignment, not sought at completion |
| **Data quality in the delivered population** | Missing identifiers weaken corroboration | Reported as data-quality observations, never as findings against entities |

## Day 31

AGT expects to be able to state, with evidence:

- how much of the sample has completed review and QA;
- the distribution across the four Government categories, for results that have
  passed QA;
- which conditions remain held pending methodology decisions, and how many;
- which sources could not answer, and for how many entities;
- whether the remaining window supports completing Task 3 to the standard, and
  if not, what would.

That last one is the point of reporting weekly. A schedule problem surfaced in
Week 4 can still be solved.
