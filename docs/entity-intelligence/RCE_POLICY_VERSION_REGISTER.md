## August 3, 2026 — verified

Four SOPs approved by the Caucuses on July 1, 2026 became effective August 3, 2026: XP Vetting Process v2.0, Treatment XP v2.0, Exchange Purposes v5.1, IAS XP v3.0. The Vetting SOP's own compliance table states "August 3, 2026" for Q/P/S. The Directory Requirements SOP v1.1 is a **separate** item, effective September 14, 2026 (two days after this verification).

## What the current effective rules actually require of organisational identity (Vetting v2.0 §4.5.2)

Legal entity name · DBA if any · corporate business address · website or attestation of none · site of care address (or a stated home/mobile/virtual designation, which "is not disqualifying") · NPI · type of health care provider · HIPAA CE evidence (Tier 1 transaction document; Tier 2 CMS directory link until 2026-12-31) · T-TRTMNT trigger description when the Principal Node is not an EHR. Representative entrants: legal name, DBA and corporate address must still represent the Participant/Subparticipant being vetted; a Representative Individual Provider's site of care must "clearly match" the Entrant's.

## What is NOT a current requirement (despite appearing in proposals)

State of incorporation/organization · existing-TEFCAID reconciliation · prior TEFCA participation and termination circumstances · 5%+ owner list · organizational chart · LEIE check of entity and owners · CLIA/NAIC as vetting identifiers · ongoing RCE audits of KYP lists. All of these are from the February 2026 changes-under-consideration paper and were not carried into Vetting v2.0.
