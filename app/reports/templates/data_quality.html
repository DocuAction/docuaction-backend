Correction authority is independent of confidence. A rule may be certain what a value should be and still have no authority to change it: identity, organisation name and relationship edits require a human at any confidence, and AUTO_SAFE is confined to deterministic, non-substantive normalisation such as whitespace, state-code case and postal-code zero-padding. Every applied correction preserves the original value alongside it.
