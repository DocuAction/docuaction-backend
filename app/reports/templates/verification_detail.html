Appendix A. Methodology, Sources and Definitions

A.1 Methodology

Entities are evaluated across six evidence dimensions. Each dimension receives one disposition regardless of how many sources or records informed it: correlated components of a single dataset are one body of evidence, not independent votes. No score, percentage or count of passing sources is derived from the dimension structure.

A.2 Data sources

ONC/RCE TEFCA directory
Primary evidence for TEFCA alignment and TEFCA relationships.
NPPES
Primary NPI identity authority. Never replaced by PECOS.
CMS PECOS Public Provider Enrollment (PPEF)
Medicare enrollment evidence. Quarterly, never real-time.
CMS Revoked Providers and Suppliers
A separate control from enrollment. A negative lookup establishes only that no active revocation record was found — it is not evidence of enrollment or of good standing.
OIG LEIE
Exclusion screening.
SAM.gov
Federal registration and debarment. Keyed on UEI, not NPI.

A.3 Definitions

Applicable dimension
A dimension the methodology requires for this entity. Dimensions recorded as not applicable are excluded from satisfied rates.
Unavailable
A source could not be reached. Never counted against an entity: an upstream outage is not evidence about the organisation.
Not found
A source answered and held no matching record.
B1–B4
B1 no discrepancy; B2 minor or administrative; B3 inexplicable; B4 non-compliant.

A.4 Known limitation

TIN and EIN are out of scope. CMS public enrollment data is not treated as exposing tax identifiers, and none are derived, scraped or inferred.
