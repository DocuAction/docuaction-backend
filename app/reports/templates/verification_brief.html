Why this verification was performed

The TEFCA Recognized Coordinating Entity supplies DocuAction with the participant and subparticipant records in scope for review. This report documents the verification of those records against authoritative federal sources, organised into six evidence dimensions, and their classification under the approved B1–B4 discrepancy methodology.

{{ scope.records_received|number }} records were in scope for this reporting period and {{ scope.records_evaluated|number }} were evaluated. {% if scope.records_held %} {{ scope.records_held|number }} record(s) were held and are not counted in the classification figures below. {% endif %}

How DocuAction verified

Each entity is evaluated across six evidence dimensions — Identity, Medicare Enrollment, Exclusion and Revocation, Address, TEFCA Alignment, and the Provider–Organization relationship. Each dimension carries its own disposition and its own applicability, and evidence is retained with the provenance needed to reproduce the determination.

Dimensions are not counted as votes. A dimension that does not apply to an entity is recorded as not applicable and excluded from the satisfied rate rather than counted against the entity, and a source that could not be reached is recorded as unavailable rather than as a finding.
